Rewilding the Punter Commons

Every Saturday...

Thousands of punters study the form.

02

CONVICTION

“I’m CERTAIN Horse 5 wins Race 3.”
“I THINK Horse 2 has a chance in Race 7.”
“Race 4? No idea.”

These are three different levels of conviction.
Every tipping platform treats them identically.

A conviction allocation captures the gradient.
The budget forces honesty about where you
actually have an edge.
03

QUADRATIC COST

The cost of expressing conviction
grows as the square.

1 vote = 1 credit
2 votes = 4 credits
3 votes = 9 credits
5 votes = 25 credits

Double the conviction.
Four times the cost.
3.3×

04

ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PROOFS

Prove something is true
without revealing anything else.

“I am a member of this community”
— without revealing which member.

“I predicted Horse 5 would win”
— proved by opening a sealed envelope after the race.

“My track record is 34% strike rate”
— without revealing who I am.
05

ANTI-COLLUSION

Five mates agree in a WhatsApp group:
“We’re all piling on Horse 3 to skew the signal.”

But any member can secretly change their vote.
And the ringleader can’t tell.

The override is invisible until the final tally.
The rational move is to silently defect
and vote honestly.

This doesn’t catch cartels.
It makes them impossible to coordinate.

Games like Among Us
and Blood on the Clocktower:

Night phase — secret actions.
Day phase — public discussion.

Some players are honest.
Some are lying.
Nobody knows who.

The drama is emergent.
That IS the engagement.
Punters who

gaming the system.
