## Slide 10 — CONVICTION

## Slide 11 — QUADRATIC COST
"Here's where it gets interesting. If you double your conviction, it costs you four times as much. This single rule means that 100 honest punters each spending 1 credit produce 10x more signal than one whale spending 100 credits. The maths structurally defends honest signal."

*If Woz looks confused*: "Think of it like backing a short-price horse. The more sure you are, the less value you get per dollar. Same principle."

---

## Slide 12 — ZERO-KNOWLEDGE PROOFS
"Don't worry about the name. Here's what it does: you can prove your track record is 34% without anyone knowing who you are. Sealed envelope. Open it after the race. Reputation becomes pure signal — were you right or not? No fame, no marketing, no bullshit."

## Slide 13 — ANTI-COLLUSION
"The cartel problem. Five mates agree to skew the signal. But here's the thing — any one of them can secretly change their vote, and the ringleader can't tell. So the rational move is to defect silently and vote honestly. The cartel can't even check if its own members complied."

*If Woz asks "has this been tested?"*: "Yes. It's called MACI. Built by the Ethereum Foundation. Production since 2020."

"Punters who would never care about zero-knowledge proofs WILL care about catching someone gaming the system. That's pub talk. That's content."

## Slide 33 — Composable Intelligence
This is the developer pitch inside the investor pitch. The conviction atom is the native data primitive.

"Every allocation, every result, every reputation delta — that's a conviction atom. They're composable. Queryable. 'Show me all high-reputation observers who allocated to roughies on wet tracks in Melbourne.' That's the API."

"Think of it like SQL for market intelligence. Filter by observer reputation, track conditions, distance, cost paid. Aggregate across communities. Compute divergence vectors — where boxes disagree on the same race."

*If Woz looks blank on "composable"*: "Lego bricks. Each brick is a single conviction — one person, one horse, one race, right or wrong. You can stack them any way you want. Across a person's history. Across a race. Across a whole carnival. Across every box in the country. The bricks are free. The ability to stack all of them — that's what we sell."

"API tiers: consensus signal is free — gets developers hooked. Divergence signal is paid — where communities disagree is where the alpha lives. Historical is paid. Anomaly detection is premium. Composable queries — full atom-level access — that's enterprise."

*If Woz asks "who buys the API?"*: "Media companies wanting race-day content. Analytics firms building models. Bookmakers wanting to understand their own customer base from the outside. Developers building apps we haven't imagined yet. Same play as Stripe — the API is the product, the developer ecosystem is the distribution."
