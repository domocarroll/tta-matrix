## Layer 1: Philosophy

> **PH1. Collective intelligence emerges from private conviction under quadratic cost, verified against ground truth.**

The structural claim: when individuals allocate scarce resources to express genuine belief, and the cost of expressing stronger belief grows quadratically, and the result is verified against objective reality, the aggregate signal outperforms any individual — including the individuals trying to manipulate it.

> **PH2. The game is the market. The market is the game.**

Play and profit are not separable. The social deduction dynamics (detecting manipulation, building trust, reading patterns) ARE the intelligence aggregation mechanism. Engagement is not layered on top — it is the mechanism itself.

> **PH3. Rewilding, not unbundling.**

The punter commons already existed — in pubs, at the track, in WhatsApp groups. Industrialised bookmaking enclosed it. The protocol doesn't take the bookmaker apart. It restores the intelligence ecosystem that was degraded. Unbundling extracts. Rewilding regenerates.

> **PH4. The wave function collapses against reality.**

Before the race, all outcomes exist in superposition. Each conviction allocation is an observation. The aggregate is the wave function of the collective mind. The race result collapses it. The system learns which observers were calibrated. Repeat. Faster vibration = faster learning = faster convergence to truth.

> **PH5. Hybrid intelligence exceeds either species alone.**

The unit of play is not the human. It is the human + their swarm. Synthetic agents process data at scale. Humans contribute irreducible insight (trackwork whispers, stable rumours, the look in the jockey's eye). Neither alone produces intelligence. Their co-evolution does.

> **PH6. The box is free. The network intelligence is the product.** *(gravity 10, emerged 2026-03-30)*

The conviction game is an open protocol, not a proprietary platform. Anyone can stand up a box — a group of mates, a racing club, a pub league. The protocol is self-hostable, the bridges are open source, the conviction engine runs on a Raspberry Pi. Revenue is captured at the aggregation layer: the intelligence that emerges when many boxes federate. No single box has the cross-box view. Only the protocol operator does. The box is the node. The network is the product. The data layer is the moat.

> **PH7. Intelligence is composable, non-fungible, and programmable.** *(gravity 10, emerged 2026-03-30)*

Each conviction atom — one observer, one allocation, one outcome, one resolution — is a non-fungible unit of verified market intelligence. Like programmable money (fungible units + composition rules + verification), programmable intelligence operates on non-fungible conviction atoms + composable queries + ZK verification. The conviction atom is the protocol's native data primitive. It flows through the network like a packet flows through the internet.

## Layer 2: Primitives

**P1. Conviction Atom**
The smallest unit of intelligence: one observer (human or synthetic) allocating one QV credit to one outcome. Irreducible.

**P2. Quadratic Cost**
The price of additional influence grows as the square: 1 vote = 1 credit, 2 votes = 4 credits, 3 votes = 9. The unique equilibrium between whale dominance (linear) and participation death (cubic).

**P3. Ground Truth Oracle**
An external fact that resolves all claims. Horse racing provides ground truth every 30 minutes, publicly, unchallengeable. The metronome of the conviction game's learning loop.

**P4. Anonymous Membership Proof**
"I am a member of this group" without revealing which member. Semaphore's core operation. The atom of cryptographic trust.

**P5. Reputation Commitment**
A hash of (identity + conviction + salt) stored before the event. The atom of provable track record. Can be opened post-race to prove what you believed.

**P6. Defection Escape**
MACI's key-change primitive: silently override a previously committed vote. Makes cartel coordination unenforceable at the protocol level.

**P7. Synthetic Observer** *(new)*
An autonomous analytical agent with genuine data streams, unique analytical persona, and real reputation consequences. Not a simulation — a player. Seeded by Pi-mono meta-agents and scaled by MiroFish swarm engine. The species that populates the ecosystem before humans arrive and co-evolves with them afterward.

**P8. Community Boundary** *(new)*
A named, branded conviction pool with its own identity, leaderboard, and membership. The atomic unit of the B2B model. Each bookmaker or sponsor gets a community boundary. Same protocol rules inside every boundary. Competition between boundaries.

**P9. Personal Swarm** *(new)*
A punter's own MiroFish-derived swarm of analytical agents, configured to reflect their analytical philosophy. The swarm learns from the punter's results and co-evolves with them. Non-transferable. The unit of play is punter + swarm. The swarm IS the punter's cognitive augmentation.

**P10. Platform Bridge** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
An adapter that connects a conviction box to an existing platform without that platform's permission. WhatsApp bridge, Sportsbet bridge, Discord bridge. The bridge scrapes/APIs data in (odds, results) and delivers intelligence out (conviction signals, leaderboard updates). Bridges are open source. Anyone can build one. You don't need Sportsbet's CDO — you need one developer who uses Sportsbet.

**P11. Conviction Receipt** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
A resolved conviction atom with full metadata: observer (hash), allocation (horse + credits), event (race + carnival), cost (quadratic), resolution (correct/incorrect), reputation delta. Non-fungible (each is unique). Composable (query/filter/aggregate). ZK-verified. The collection of your receipts IS your provable track record. The aggregate of all receipts across all boxes IS the protocol's intelligence.

**P12. Federation Protocol** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
The mechanism by which independent conviction boxes discover each other and share conviction atoms. Self-hosted boxes opt in to data sharing in exchange for cross-box intelligence. Each box retains sovereignty over its membership and rules. The federation creates the aggregate that no single box can produce. Isomorphic to: Matrix federation, email SMTP, internet BGP.

## Layer 3: Features

**F1. Night Phase — Conviction Allocation**
Input: observer's credit budget (human + swarm) + race field data. Output: encrypted QV allocation across horses. Uses P1, P2, P4. Private, simultaneous, ZK-hidden.

**F2. Dawn — Ground Truth Settlement**
Input: race result (scraper). Output: all allocations resolved against truth. Uses P3. Automatic. Tipster stats update. Predictions settle. Credits rebalance. Synthetic observer models recalibrate.

**F3. Day Phase — Forensic Pattern Reveal**
Input: aggregate allocation statistics. Output: anomaly signals surfaced to community WITHOUT individual vote revelation. Uses P4. "This race had unusual conviction concentration."

**F4. Conviction Reveal (Optional)**
Input: observer's choice to open their commitment post-race. Output: verified proof of allocation. Uses P5. Social currency — "I called it." Works for humans and synthetic observers alike.

**F5. Reputation Accumulation**
Input: history of allocations vs ground truth. Output: ZK-provable track record. Uses P5, P4. Reputation is public. Identity is private. Applies to both human and synthetic observers.

**F6. Cartel Detection & Decay**
Input: statistical patterns across many races. Output: reputation decay for systematically wrong or correlated accounts. Uses P3 + temporal analysis. The iterated game's immune system.

**F7. Tip Extraction Pipeline**
Input: tip sheet image. Output: structured tip data. Uses Claude vision. Already built (M0). Feeds human-generated intelligence into the ecosystem.

**F8. Tiered Channel Delivery**
Input: aggregated conviction signal. Output: tier-appropriate intelligence. WhatsApp → Matrix → Premium. Same signal, different resolution.

**F9. Seasonal Credit Reset**
Input: end-of-season standings. Output: partial credit rebalancing. Fresh game state. Prevents freezing (SOC maintenance).

**F10. Personal Swarm Configuration** *(new)*
Input: punter's analytical preferences (data weightings, risk profile, specialisations). Output: configured MiroFish swarm of 10-100+ synthetic agents tuned to the punter's philosophy. Uses P9. The swarm produces conviction signals the punter reviews, adjusts, and composes with their own. Co-evolves through the season.

**F11. Community Instantiation** *(new)*
Input: brand identity + sport + configuration. Output: a new community boundary (P8) with its own leaderboard, swarm population, and membership. Uses P8. The deployment unit of the B2B model. Each bookmaker or sponsor gets one (or many).

**F12. Inter-Community Competition** *(new)*
Input: multiple community boundaries playing the same races. Output: cross-community accuracy rankings. "Sportsbet's community called 7/10, Ladbrokes got 5." Uses P8. The content and engagement engine of the B2B model.

**F13. Meta-Intelligence Aggregation** *(new)*
Input: conviction signals from ALL communities. Output: cross-community meta-signal visible only to the protocol operator. The information advantage no single community has. Divergence between communities IS the signal.

**F14. Synthetic Ecosystem Seeding** *(new)*
Input: Pi-mono meta-agents (7 founding species) + MiroFish swarm generation. Output: 1,000+ synthetic observers with diverse analytical personas, active from day zero. Uses P7. The cold start solver. The ecosystem is alive before the first human arrives.

**F15. Self-Hosted Box Instantiation** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
Input: `npx conviction init --name "Friday Night Form" --bridge whatsapp`. Output: a running conviction box on any machine (Raspberry Pi, $5 VPS, laptop). Uses P10, P12. Open source. No permission needed. The protocol spreads like email — anyone can run a server.

**F16. Carnival Activation** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
Input: brand + event + duration. Output: a time-bounded conviction box that opens for a carnival (Melbourne Cup week, The Everest) and closes after. Uses P8. Creates urgency (FOMO), mirrors physical corporate box model, scopes engagement to high-intensity event windows. Marketing spend for sponsors, not infrastructure commitment.

**F17. Conviction Intelligence API** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
Input: API query over federated conviction atoms. Output: composed intelligence signals. Uses P11, P12. Tiers: `/signals/consensus` (free) → `/signals/divergence` (paid — where boxes disagree) → `/signals/historical` (paid) → `/signals/anomaly` (premium) → `/atoms/compose` (enterprise — composable queries). The divergence signal is the killer product.

**F18. Atom Composition Engine** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
Input: query DSL over conviction receipts. Output: composed intelligence primitives. Filter by observer reputation, event, track conditions, distance, cost paid. Aggregate across communities. Compute divergence vectors. This is SQL for market intelligence. Uses P11.

## Layer 4: Patterns

**PA1. The Social Deduction Loop**
Allocate (Night) → Settle (Dawn) → Forensics (Day) → Discuss (Dusk) → Allocate...
Isomorphic to: BotC night/day, Among Us task/meeting, Mafia phases.
30-minute cycles. Season-long arcs. The breath of the conviction market.

**PA2. The Mycelium Growth Pattern**
Invisible → Valuable → Visible → Defensible.
Isomorphic to: punk underground → mainstream. Bitcoin cypherpunks → global adoption.
Communication strategy: don't talk about fight club. The product spreads through value, not marketing.

**PA3. The Reputation Flywheel**
Accurate conviction → Credits earned → More influence → Better signal → More trust → More participants → Better aggregation → Repeat.
Applies to both humans and synthetic observers. The flywheel spins faster with hybrid intelligence.

**PA4. The Quadratic Firewall**
Distributed defense is 3.3x more efficient than concentrated attack per credit spent.
Geometry defends the honest signal: the paraboloid cost surface means attackers push uphill.

**PA5. The Wedge-to-Protocol-to-Platform Pattern** *(evolved)*
```
Vertical product (TTA racing)
  → Proven mechanism (QV+ZK conviction game)
    → Domain-agnostic protocol (MACI-for-predictions)
      → B2B platform (N bookmakers × M sports × P sponsors)
        → Meta-intelligence layer (cross-community signal)
```
Each stage validates the next. Each stage generates revenue for the next.

**PA6. The N×M×P Community Matrix** *(new)*
```
N bookmakers × M sports × P sponsors = N×M×P community boundaries
Same protocol. Different brand, audience, sport.
Marginal cost per cell → near zero.
Inter-cell competition → engagement engine.
Cross-cell aggregation → meta-intelligence moat.
```
Isomorphic to: WoW servers (same engine, different populations, cross-realm events).

**PA7. The Wave Function Collapse Loop** *(new)*
```
Superposition (pre-race, all outcomes possible)
  → Observations (human + synthetic conviction allocations)
    → Wave function (aggregate probability distribution)
      → Collapse (race result, ground truth)
        → Recalibration (reputation update, model adjustment)
          → New superposition (next race)
```
The conviction game vibrates at racing frequency. Faster vibration = faster learning.
Horse racing's 30-minute loop is the fastest conviction clock available.

**PA8. The Co-Evolutionary Ecology** *(new)*
```
Synthetic agents analyse data → produce baseline conviction
Humans add irreducible insight → conviction becomes richer
Race collapses wave function → ground truth tests all observers
Synthetic agents recalibrate → models improve
Humans adjust strategy → intuition informed by model performance
Personal swarms co-evolve with their human → unique hybrid intelligence
Both improve → ecosystem intelligence compounds
```
Not human OR machine. Human WITH machine. OOGI at every level: individual (punter + swarm), community (humans + synthetic observers), platform (communities + meta-intelligence).

**PA9. The Rewilding Pattern** *(new)*
```
Degraded commons (industrialised bookmaking enclosed collective intelligence)
  → Remove artificial constraints (break information asymmetry)
    → Reintroduce keystone species (synthetic observers as analytical diversity)
      → Trust emergence (quadratic cost + ground truth + reputation)
        → Ecosystem health compounds (more species → more resilience → more intelligence)
```
Isomorphic to: ecological rewilding (reintroduce wolves → river changes course).
The protocol is the keystone species. The commons was always there. It was degraded.
We're not building something new. We're letting something grow back.

**PA10. The Activation Pattern (Sportsbet vs Ladbrokes)** *(new)*
```
Grassroots campaign: "Which bookmaker's punters are smarter?"
  → Punters self-identify bookmaker tribe
    → Conviction game runs head-to-head for 4 weeks
      → Results published weekly (content, drama, tribal identity)
        → Data accumulated (proof of mechanism)
          → Walk into CDO's office with receipts
            → B2B deal: "This is what your punters did without you"
```
The proof-of-concept that sells the B2B platform without needing permission.
Tribal identity (already exists in AU punter culture) is the viral mechanism.

**PA11. The Open Protocol Pattern (Linux, not Salesforce)** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
```
Protocol is open source, self-hostable, free
  → Developers build bridges (WhatsApp, Sportsbet, Discord)
    → Anyone stands up a box (mates, clubs, pubs)
      → Boxes federate (opt in to data sharing)
```

email (free protocol, Gmail sells convenience + spam filtering = meta-intelligence).
The protocol is unkillable because it's self-hostable. The moat is the aggregate, not the instance.

**PA12. The Carnival Pop-Up Pattern** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
```
Brand sponsors a digital corporate box for a carnival (4 days)
  → Box opens Tuesday, closes Saturday
  → Time-bounded leaderboard: "Who called the Cup?"
```

**PA13. The Composable Intelligence Pattern** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)*
```
Conviction atoms generated by all boxes across all events
  → Federated into a global conviction graph
```

Isomorphic to: programmable money (fungible units + smart contracts + verification),
but for intelligence (non-fungible atoms + composable queries + ZK verification).

## Layer 5: Strategy

> **S1.** Enter the conviction intelligence market through the game layer, not the technology layer. Own the community dynamic. The cryptography is free. The social fabric that makes it meaningful is not.

> **S2.** Rewild, don't unbundle. We're not taking bookmakers apart. We're restoring the commons they enclosed. This affects language, positioning, legal framing, and product design.

> **S3.** Seed the ecosystem with synthetic intelligence from day zero. The cold start problem dissolves when 1,000 analytical agents are already playing. Humans walk into a living game, not an empty room.

> **S4.** Give every punter their own swarm. The unit of play is punter + swarm. Personal cognitive augmentation is the premium product. The swarm co-evolves with the human. Non-transferable. This IS the retention moat.

> **S5.** *(evolved 2026-03-30)* Open the protocol, capture value at the aggregate. Don't sell boxes to bookmakers — let anyone stand up a box. Bookmakers are ONE customer segment. Groups of mates, racing clubs, pub leagues, podcast communities — all box operators. Revenue comes from the managed service (convenience), the data API (intelligence), and the composable primitives (developer platform). The meta-intelligence layer (cross-box aggregation) is exclusively ours and gets more valuable with every box added to the network.

> **S6.** Prove the mechanism underground, then surface with receipts. Self-hosted boxes spreading pub-to-pub IS the underground. By the time the industry notices, 500 boxes are running, the data is flowing, and the network is too distributed to kill.

> **S7.** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)* Sell the view, not the box. The box is a node. The network of boxes is the product. The cross-box divergence signal (where communities disagree on the same race) is intelligence that no single box can produce. Monetize this exclusively through the API. Self-hosted boxes that opt in to data sharing get cross-box intelligence in return — a fair exchange that grows the network.

> **S8.** *(gravity 10, 2026-03-30)* Build for developers, not CDOs. The Sportsbet integration is a developer building a sidequest plugin, not a CDO signing a contract. `npx conviction init` + `npx conviction bridge sportsbet` = a running box in minutes. Developer adoption drives network growth. Enterprise deals follow developer traction, not the other way around.

**What we are NOT doing**: bookmaker, data analytics tool, social network, crypto token project, fighting the oligopoly publicly, unbundling anything, building a proprietary platform.

**What we ARE doing**: rewilding the epistemic commons through an open conviction protocol played by hybrid human-synthetic intelligence, where boxes are self-hostable nodes, bridges connect to existing platforms, and the protocol operator captures value at the aggregation layer — the intelligence that emerges when many boxes federate.

**Six-layer moat** *(evolved 2026-03-30)*:
1. Community (social fabric inside each box, can't copy)
2. Data (months of conviction-vs-truth across all boxes, can't fake)
3. Mechanism (QV+ZK game design, hard to replicate)
4. Personal swarms (co-evolved with individual punters, non-transferable)
5. Network effects (more boxes → richer aggregate → more boxes, self-reinforcing)
6. Meta-intelligence (cross-box aggregation, exclusively ours, the ONLY thing that can't be self-hosted)
