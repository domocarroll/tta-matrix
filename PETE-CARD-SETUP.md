# Adding your card to TipAnalyser — a quick guide

Hi Pete — this sets up the billing that powers TipAnalyser's reader. It takes
about 5 minutes, once. You'll add a card and switch on "top up automatically"
so it never runs out and you never have to think about it again.

The cost is small — roughly **$1.50 a week** for a normal batch of sheets. There's
no subscription and a hard monthly cap is already set, so it can't surprise you.

---

## Step 1 — Open the invite

You'll get an email from **Anthropic** with the subject line about being invited
to an organisation. Open it and click **Accept invitation**.

- If it asks you to create an account, use **peter@blacmacproductions.com** and pick
  a password. (This is the account that runs the reader — keep the login handy.)
- If you already have an Anthropic login, just sign in.

## Step 2 — Go to Billing

Once you're in, look on the **left-hand side** for **Settings**, then click **Billing**.

(If you don't see it straight away, click your organisation name at the top-left and
make sure you're in **"TipAnalyser — Pete Blackburn"**.)

## Step 3 — Add your card

- Click **Add payment method** (or **Add card**).
- Type in your card details and save.

## Step 4 — Switch on automatic top-up (so it never stops)

- On the same Billing page, find **Auto-reload** (sometimes called "automatic
  recharge").
- Turn it **on**. A sensible setting: **add $20 when the balance drops below $5.**
- Save.

That's it. From now on it tops itself up a little when it gets low, your card
covers it, and you don't have to do anything.

## Step 5 — Buy a little to start (optional)

If there's a **Buy credits** button, add **$20** now so it's ready to go. (If Dom
already put a starter amount on, you can skip this.)

---

## A few things to put your mind at ease

- **It's tiny.** A normal week is about $1.50. $20 lasts roughly three months.
- **No subscription.** You only pay for what the reader actually uses.
- **It's capped.** A monthly limit is already in place so it can never run away.
- **Your card, your control.** You can see usage and change or remove the card any
  time on that same Billing page.

## If anything looks different or you get stuck

Screens change sometimes, and that's fine — anything that says **Billing**,
**Add card**, or **Auto-reload** is the right thing. If you're unsure at any point,
**stop and send Dom a screenshot** rather than guessing. Happy to jump on a call.
